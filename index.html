Notable Incidents

FROM THE PURSUE RELEASE
1947
ROSWELL, NM
Roswell Crash Report
The FBI's 18-document case file (62-HQ-83894), covering unidentified objects and "flying discs" from 1947–1968, was released with fewer redactions than prior versions. It contains eyewitness testimonies and photographic evidence from sites including Oak Ridge, TN.
FBI FILE NEWLY DECLASSIFIED PHOTOGRAPHIC EVIDENCE
2004
OFF SAN DIEGO, CA
"Tic Tac" — USS Nimitz Encounter
U.S. Navy pilots from the USS Nimitz carrier group encountered a 40-foot white oval object that descended from 80,000 feet to near sea level instantaneously, with no visible propulsion. Officially confirmed by DoD in 2020 as authentic UAP footage.
CONFIRMED DOD MULTIPLE WITNESSES RADAR CORROBORATED
2022
IRAQ
Iraq Small UAP — Military Report
Internal military memo describes "one possible small UAP" during a military operation in Iraq. The object crossed a U.S. aircraft's surveillance systems at high rate of speed while the crew was engaged with an unrelated target.
MILITARY MEMO PURSUIT TRANCHE 1
2023
WESTERN U.S.
"Orbs Launching Orbs" — Law Enforcement
Over two days, multiple federal law enforcement officers independently reported strange incidents involving orbs. One report described "orbs launching other orbs." AARO (All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office) describes this as among the most compelling cases in their possession.
FEDERAL LAW ENFORCEMENT MULTIPLE WITNESSES AARO: MOST COMPELLING
2025
WESTERN U.S.
Infrared Detection — Dec 2025
FBI infrared still image (black hot) captured from a U.S. military platform showing unidentified object(s) over the western United States. Released in the May 2026 PURSUE tranche with fewer redactions than prior versions.
